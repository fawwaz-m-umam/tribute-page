Sultan Mehmed II

Ottoman Sultan who conquered Constantinople in 1453.

Short Biography

Mehmed II was the seventh Sultan of the Ottoman Empire and one of the most influential rulers in history. Born in 1432, he is best known for conquering Constantinople in 1453 at the age of 21, an event that marked the end of the Byzantine Empire. Because of this achievement, he earned the title “Mehmed the Conqueror.” He was also known for his intelligence, leadership, and interest in science, art, and education, helping the Ottoman Empire grow into a powerful world empire.

Timeline of Key Events

  • Early Life and First Reign (1432–1446)

    • March 30, 1432: Born in Edirne, son of Sultan Murad II.
    • 1444: Appointed ruler at age 12 following his father's temporary abdication; faced significant European Crusade threats during this first, brief reign.
    • 1446: His father, Murad II, resumed the throne, and Mehmed returned to Manisa to continue his education.

  • Consolidation and the Conquest (1451–1453)

    • February 1451: Mehmed II succeeded his father as sultan for the second time.
    • 1452: Built the Rumelihisari (Rumelian Fortress) on the Bosphorus to sever Constantinople's supply lines.
    • April 6 – May 29, 1453: Led the Siege of Constantinople.
    • May 29, 1453: Captured Constantinople (later known as Istanbul), marking the end of the Byzantine Empire. He subsequently made it the new Ottoman capital and began to rebuild the city.

  • Expansion and Campaigns (1454–1480)

    • 1454–1459: Campaigns in Serbia.
    • 1456: Besieged Belgrade, where he was defeated by John Hunyadi.
    • 1460–1461: Conquered the Morea (Peloponnese) and Trebizond, dismantling the last remnants of Byzantine rule.
    • 1462: Defeated Vlad III (Vlad the Impaler) in Wallachia.
    • 1463: Conquered Bosnia.
    • 1463–1479: Long war against Venice over Aegean possessions.
    • 1465: Oversees the construction of the Topkapi Palace.
    • 1475: Subjugated the Crimean Khanate.
    • 1476: Campaign against Moldavia.
    • 1480: Launched an expedition to Italy, taking Otranto.

  • Final Days (1481)

    • May 3, 1481: Died at Hunkârçayırı, near Maltepe, while on a new military campaign, likely heading towards Asia.
